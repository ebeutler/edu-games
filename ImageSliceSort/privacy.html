Data privacy

Your images remain on your device

Images selected from your files or camera are processed entirely within your browser. They are used only to create and display the sorting visualization.

The images are never transmitted to a server, uploaded, or shared with a third party. Image Slice Sort does not store them in cookies, local storage, or any other persistent browser storage.

The browser keeps the current image in memory only while the application is open. Selecting another image, reloading the page, or closing the page removes it from the application.
